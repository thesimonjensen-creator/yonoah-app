Getting started

  1. A parent or carer opens Yo Noah on their iPhone and taps Set Up a New Family (each iPhone needs to be signed in to iCloud).
  2. In Manage → Family & iPhones → Add an iPhone, choose the child's iPhone. A QR code appears.
  3. On the child's iPhone, open Yo Noah, tap Join a Family and scan the code.
  4. Add Alerts and Tasks in Manage. They appear on the child's iPhone in seconds.

Tips

  • Leave Yo Noah running in the background (don't swipe it away) so notifications arrive promptly.
  • On the child's iPhone, allow Time Sensitive notifications for Yo Noah so Alerts show during Do Not Disturb. Sounds play at the ringer volume; on silent the iPhone vibrates.
  • Pull down on the Reviewer Home Screen to check for changes straight away.
