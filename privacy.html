Privacy Policy

Yo Noah · last updated 16 October 2026

HomeSupportTerms of Use
In short: Yo Noah keeps your family's information on your family's iPhones and in your family's own iCloud storage. The developer never receives it. There is no advertising of our own, no analytics and no tracking. YouTube Time uses YouTube API Services, so searches and videos go to YouTube (Google).

Who we are

Yo Noah is made by Simon Jensen (“we”). Contact: yonoahsupport@icloud.com.

What Yo Noah holds

  • The child's first name.
  • Tasks – names, steps, schedules, time allowed and photo instructions.
  • Alerts – pictures chosen from the photo library and voice recordings.
  • Reviewers (the grown-ups) – name, phone number and, if given, email address and picture.
  • Rewards – names, pictures and progress.
  • The child's Task history – when Tasks were done, late, left for the day or reviewed – and streaks.
  • Photos the child takes to show a Task is done, and messages from Reviewers (review messages and Encouragement).

Where it is kept and who can see it

  • Only on the iPhones in your Family and in your Family's own private iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit), under your Family's own Apple IDs. It is shared between your Family's iPhones using Apple's iCloud sharing.
  • Only the people in your Family – the child and the Reviewers – can see it. We (the developer) cannot see or access it.
  • Some settings are stored on each iPhone (for example which notifications have already been shown). Photos are kept inside Yo Noah and are never saved to the iPhone's photo library.

How long it is kept

  • Photos are deleted from every iPhone one month after they were taken.
  • The Task history is kept so progress and streaks can be shown, until a Reviewer uses “Remove Child from Family”, which permanently deletes the child's history, photos, Reward progress and YouTube Time.
  • Removing a Reviewer's iPhone ends that iPhone's access. Deleting the app and the Family's iCloud data removes everything.

YouTube Time (YouTube API Services)

Yo Noah uses YouTube API Services to let the child search for and watch videos as a reward. When YouTube Time is used, the search text is sent to YouTube to find videos, and videos are played in YouTube's own embedded player (in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode). YouTube (Google) may collect information, including through cookies or similar technology on the device, and may show its own adverts. This is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. By using YouTube Time you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Yo Noah does not use a YouTube or Google account and does not store your searches or watch history.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

Yo Noah has no advertising of its own, does not use analytics and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell or share any information.

Permissions

  • Camera – photos that show a Task is done, and scanning the QR code that joins an iPhone to the Family.
  • Microphone – recording an Alert's voice message.
  • Photo library – choosing pictures for Alerts, Rewards and Reviewers (only the picture chosen is used).
  • Notifications – Alerts, Late, review and Encouragement messages.

If a permission is turned off, only the feature that needs it stops working.

Children

Yo Noah is set up and managed by a parent or carer (a Reviewer). A child's iPhone joins only when a Reviewer shows it a QR code. Only the child's first name is used. Nothing about the child is sent to us.

Your rights

Because your information is held in your own iCloud and devices, you can see, change or delete it at any time in the app (Manage), or delete it by removing the app and its iCloud data (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage). If you have any question about your information or this policy, contact yonoahsupport@icloud.com.
